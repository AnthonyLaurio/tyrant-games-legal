Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Related document: Terms and Conditions

This Privacy Policy describes how Tyrant Studios LLC ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects information when you use the Tyrant mobile application, related game services, and this website (collectively, the "Services").

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Your account ID, username, linked sign-in provider, and email address if you sign in with Apple or Google or create an account with an email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email," we may receive a private relay email address instead of your personal email address.
  • Guest Account and Device Information: A persistent device identifier used to create and maintain a guest account, keep you signed in, prevent duplicate guest accounts, and link your progress to your device. On iOS this is Apple's identifier for vendor; on Android it is the Android ID. We do not collect your device's name or any advertising identifier.
  • Gameplay and Progress Data: Your server selections, castles, buildings, troops, heroes, resources, inventory, progression, battle reports, quests, achievements, event participation, matchmaking, and other saved game state.
  • Social and Multiplayer Data: Your guild membership, guild applications and invitations, leaderboard positions, profile information visible to other players, and in-game chat messages or other player-to-player communications that you send through the Services.
  • Notification Data: Push notification tokens, notification preferences, and information needed to send gameplay-related alerts such as construction completion, troop activity, event alerts, and guild activity.
  • Support and Communications: Information you provide when you contact support, including your email address and the contents of your message.
  • Purchase and Transaction Data: When you make in-app purchases, including consumable items and auto-renewing subscriptions, we receive transaction identifiers, receipt or purchase tokens, product identifiers, purchase and renewal status, and the entitlements associated with your account. Payment processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play, and we do not receive your credit card number, full payment instrument details, or billing address. Apple or Google may share country, currency, tax, and refund or chargeback information with us as part of operating the in-app billing system.
  • Technical and Security Data: Technical information needed to operate and secure the Services, such as authentication status, app version, platform, IP address, and service logs generated when you use the app.
  • Login and Session History: For each sign-in, the device identifier, a salted one-way hash of your IP address (never the IP address itself), platform, app version, sign-in method, and whether it succeeded; and the times you connect to and disconnect from each game server. We use this to detect cheating, account sharing, and players running multiple accounts against the rules.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: When the app crashes or hits an error, a report with the error message and code location, app version and update, platform, operating system version, device model (for example "iPhone 15"), and the screens and game features you used just before the error. We remove email addresses and sign-in tokens from error text before storing it.
  • Performance Data: Measurements of how smoothly the app runs, such as moments the app froze and for how long, memory use, how long the current session has lasted, whether your device's graphics support the map, and the screen you were on, along with platform, operating system version, and device model.
  • Feedback: When you send feedback in the app, your message together with the same app and device details included in crash reports, so we can reproduce the problem.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage guest and linked accounts
  • Authenticate users through Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email and password
  • Save, sync, and restore gameplay progress across sessions and devices where supported
  • Operate multiplayer systems, guild features, chat, leaderboards, events, and matchmaking
  • Send push notifications and maintain your notification settings
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests
  • Process and validate in-app purchases, deliver consumable items and subscription benefits, manage subscription status and renewals, handle refunds and chargebacks, and comply with tax and recordkeeping obligations
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, cheating, and multi-account abuse, enforce our terms, and debug service issues
  • Find and fix crashes, errors, and performance problems
  • Improve game balance, reliability, and overall service performance
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, players, and services

Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your information on these bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to create your account, run the game, save your progress, operate multiplayer features, send the notifications you allow, and deliver purchases.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Services secure and fair (login and session history), to find and fix crashes and performance problems (crash, diagnostic, and performance data), and to improve the game. You can object to this processing by contacting us, and you can turn off crash and performance reports in the app at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep transaction records for tax and accounting, and to respond to lawful requests.
  • Consent: for push notifications, which you can turn off in your device settings or in the app.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use personal information for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising. We may share information only as described below:

  • Service Providers: We may share information with vendors and infrastructure providers that host, secure, support, or operate the Services on our behalf. These include our cloud hosting providers, Expo (app updates and push notification delivery), and RevenueCat (in-app purchase and subscription management, which receives your account ID and purchase records). They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Authentication Providers: If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, those providers process sign-in data in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Notification Providers: Push notification tokens may be shared with service providers needed to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Payment and Billing Platforms: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. These platforms collect and process payment information directly under their own terms and privacy policies, and they share limited transaction data with us as needed to grant entitlements, manage subscriptions, and handle refunds.
  • Other Players: Your username, guild affiliation, leaderboard rank, profile information shown in-game, and messages you send may be visible to other players as part of the core functionality of the game.
  • Legal or Safety Reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to investigate, prevent, or address fraud, abuse, security, or safety issues.
  • Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information in our Services, including:

  • Encrypted transmission of data over HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure storage of authentication credentials on supported devices
  • Access controls and operational safeguards intended to limit unauthorized access

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep these records for limited periods:

  • Login and session history: 90 days
  • Individual crash and error reports: 30 days; grouped error summaries: 90 days
  • Performance data and service logs: up to 30 days
  • Resolved feedback: 180 days after it is resolved

We retain account, gameplay, social, and support data for as long as needed to provide the Services, maintain your account, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

If you request account deletion, your account is scheduled for permanent deletion after a 7-day waiting period. During that period, you may be able to cancel the deletion request by logging back in. After the deletion period expires, your account and associated personal data are deleted or anonymized, subject to any information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping purposes.

We may retain aggregated or de-identified gameplay and service data that does not reasonably identify you.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the personal data we hold about you. You may also have the right to appeal or limit certain processing in some jurisdictions.

You can also control certain information directly in the app, such as notification preferences, account settings, and whether the app sends crash and performance reports (Settings). To exercise a privacy request, contact us using the information below.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would give you a right to limit it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may also object to processing based on our legitimate interests, request that we restrict processing, request a copy of your data in a portable format, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Tyrant Studios LLC is the controller of your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without legally valid authorization where required. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will investigate and take appropriate action.

International Data Processing

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than the one where you live. By using the Services, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions that may have different data protection rules than your jurisdiction.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice in the app or through other appropriate means.
